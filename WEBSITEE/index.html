About This Project

About the College of Computer Studies

The Trimex College of Computer Studies provides students with opportunities to develop technical knowledge, creativity, problem-solving skills, and practical experience in information technology and computing.

  • Project-based learning in modern computer laboratories
  • Research and capstone guidance from faculty
  • On-the-job training and OJT monitoring
  • Digital campus services through CCS-MS
